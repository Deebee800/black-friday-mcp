What to look for in a monitor for a Mac

Check these five things before you buy.

Pixel density
macOS looks sharpest near 218 PPI, at exactly 2x. A 32-inch 6K lands there too. A 32-inch 4K drops to about 140 PPI, and text looks softer.
One cable for a MacBook Pro
Match the monitor's watts to Apple's charger for your model. The 16-inch ships with 140 W and may drain slowly on 90 W under heavy load.
Mac mini ports
A Mac mini has its own power supply and needs no power from the monitor. Recent models have HDMI and Thunderbolt. For 5K, use Thunderbolt.
Number of displays
Check Apple's tech specs for your chip before you buy two.
Finish and stand
Glossy looks crisp but reflects lamps. Matte cuts glare. Get height adjustment or a VESA mount, since some Mac-focused screens only tilt.

Questions developers ask

Is 1440p or 4K better for Mac?

+

4K. At 27 inches it shows the workspace of 1440p with 1.5 times the pixel density. A 24-inch 4K at "looks like 1920 x 1080" is an exact 2x.

Why does my external monitor look blurry on a Mac?

+

Usually too few pixels. macOS dropped subpixel font smoothing in 2018, so 1080p and 1440p text looks soft. If a 4K screen is blurry, check System Settings → Displays and swap an old HDMI adapter for USB-C.

Does a Mac mini need a USB-C monitor?

+

No. HDMI works, and a USB-C to DisplayPort cable runs a DisplayPort monitor from a Thunderbolt port. A USB-C monitor with a hub adds USB ports on the same cable.

Can I use a MacBook Pro with the lid closed?

+

Yes. Apple calls it closed-display mode. Connect the display, an external keyboard and mouse, and power, then close the lid.
