What to check before you buy

Five questions settle most programmer gifts.

Mac or Windows
Current MacBooks have no USB-A ports, so pick a hub or SSD with a USB-C plug. Most work on both systems.
Desk space
A 36 x 12 inch desk mat fits a keyboard and mouse. A light bar sits on top of the monitor and takes no desk space. For a curved screen, get a bar made for curves.
What they already own
Ask a partner or teammate, or look at their desk on a video call.
Delivery by Dec 25
Check the delivery date before you pay. Third-party sellers can take longer to ship.
Gift receipt and returns
Mark the order as a gift so the packing slip shows no prices. In past years Amazon extended holiday returns to January 31. Check this year's dates.

Questions gift buyers ask

What do you get a programmer who has everything?

+

Something they use daily but would not buy themselves, like a spare USB-C charger for their bag.

Is a mechanical keyboard a good gift for a programmer?

+

Only if they asked for one. Then match the size they use now, Mac or Windows keycaps, and the switches they like.

What is a good gift for a programmer who works from home?

+

Something for video calls. Headphones with a good mic make them easier to hear. A small light in front helps if a window sits behind them.
