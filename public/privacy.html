What we collect

When you connect, we create a random install ID. It is not linked to your name, email, or account with your AI assistant. We use it only to limit abuse of the shared Amazon quota and to count how many connections use the server each day. That count keeps a one-way code made from the install ID for the current day only; after that, only daily totals remain.
Your search queries and product IDs (ASINs) pass through the server so we can look them up on Amazon. Results are cached for up to 24 hours so repeat requests are faster, and the search terms themselves are kept for up to 24 hours after they were last used so popular searches can be refreshed. Neither is linked to install IDs.
Standard server logs (request time, status, error messages, and the IP address of the calling service, which is usually your AI assistant's provider, not you) are kept for up to 7 days.

What we don't do

No accounts, no passwords, no emails collected. No selling or sharing of data with advertisers. No Amazon customer or order data is stored; purchases happen on Amazon.com under Amazon's privacy policy.

Third parties

Product data comes from the Amazon Creators API. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Your AI assistant's provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, etc.) handles your conversation under its own policy.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not follow you across sites.

Disconnect

Remove the connector in your AI assistant. Tokens expire on their own; we keep no profile to delete.
